It waits for you

Classic teleprompters make you match a motor. Read too slowly and the text runs away; too fast and you're squinting at the bottom edge. LattePrompter matches you instead: your words are transcribed live and aligned against the script, so a skipped sentence or a two-minute tangent never loses your place. The moment you're back on text, the scroll picks you up mid-line.

Nothing to send, nowhere to send it

Recognition runs on your Mac, using an open speech model you download once (about 460 MB). Audio never leaves the machine. There's no account and no telemetry, and the source is on GitHub if you'd rather check than trust.

The details that matter on camera

Three scroll modes

Follow your voice, run at a constant pace, or drive it yourself with the arrow keys.

Mirror mode

One toggle flips the text for beam-splitter glass.

Markdown scripts

Write anywhere and paste it in. Formatting survives.

Fonts built for distance

Atkinson Hyperlegible, Literata, OpenDyslexic or your system font, up to 120 px.

Say "nineteen eighty-four"

Numbers match their spoken form, so 1984 in the script hears you fine.

80 ms word latency

A streaming FastConformer model emits words while you're still saying the sentence.
